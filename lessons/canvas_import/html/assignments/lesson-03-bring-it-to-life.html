What to do

Give your character motion that feels like it belongs to that character.

  • Set a velocity in setup() and call move() in draw(), in the right order.
  • Watch it wrap around the screen edges at least once.
  • Tune the speed until it feels right for your character. A heavy monster and a darting bug should not move at the same speed.
  • Set a background color that matches the mood.

What to turn in

  1. Your sketch folder, zipped.
  2. A short screen recording (a few seconds) so the motion is visible — or a screenshot plus a sentence describing how it moves.
  3. Your answer to Show your thinking below.

To zip your sketch: Sketch → Show Sketch Folder, go up one level, then right-click the sketch folder and compress it.
