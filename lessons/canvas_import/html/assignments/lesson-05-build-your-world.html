What to do

Build the place your game happens in.  Most of this lesson is design.  The code is small, and the world is the point.

  • Add terrain with at least one Block.  Remember its x, y is the top-left corner.
  • Add at least one decoration made from a sprite class in its own tab -- the Cloud, or one you wrote.
  • Order your draw() calls on purpose so the layering is right -- background first, player last.
  • Give the world a theme and carry it through:  background color, terrain, decorations, and your coin.
  • Be able to point at one draw-order decision you made on purpose.

What to turn in

  1. Your sketch folder, zipped (all tabs).
  2. A screenshot of your world -- make it one you would want to show someone.
  3. Your answer to Show your thinking below.
