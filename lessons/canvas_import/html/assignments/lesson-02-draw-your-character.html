What to do

Build a character that is yours. This is the character you will use for the rest of the unit, so make it one you actually want to look at for nine lessons.

  • Create a MyCharacter tab with class MyCharacter extends Blob.
  • Override drawSprite() and build your character out of shapes.
  • Anchor every shape to pos.x and pos.y. This matters — next lesson your character starts moving, and hard-coded numbers will leave your face behind.
  • Make it recognizably yours. Not the starter blob with a different color.

What to turn in

  1. Your sketch folder, zipped (both tabs).
  2. A screenshot of your character.
  3. Your answer to Show your thinking below.

To zip your sketch: Sketch → Show Sketch Folder, go up one level, then right-click the sketch folder and compress it.
