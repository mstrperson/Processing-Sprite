What to do

Take control of your character. Try both styles, then commit to one and tune it until it feels good.

  • Get both control styles working, at least briefly. You need to feel the difference to choose well.
  • Pick the one that fits the game you want to build, and tune it — the chase speed, or how it handles the coasting.
  • Add one extra piece of input if you can: a mousePressed() ability, or a second blob moving on its own.

Where am I?

Standard
Control the character with followMouse() or keyboardControl(), and distinguish continuous input from event input.
Getting StartedMy character does not respond to input yet. Next step: for followMouse, set a speed with setVelocity() in setup() — it needs a speed above 0 to move at all. For keys, make sure keyboardControl() is inside keyPressed(), not draw(), and that you clicked inside the sketch window first.

Made It Mine ⭐…and I picked the control style that fits my game and tuned it — a speed that feels right, or a mousePressed() that does something fun.
Went Beyond…and I combined inputs (keyboard control plus a mousePressed() ability), or added a second blob that moves on its own while I steer the player.
