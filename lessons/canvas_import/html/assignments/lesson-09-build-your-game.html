What to do

Build a complete, playable game starring your character. This is the capstone — the goal is a finished thing you are proud of, not a perfect one.

  • Combine movement, control, lists, collisions, state, and your world into one game.
  • Write both a win condition and a lose condition. They should look different when they happen.
  • Make restart work, using restartGame() to reset by re-running your setup steps.
  • Replace every placeholder with something that belongs in your world.
  • Add at least one thing the lesson did not ask for — a rule, a mechanic, an ability that is your own idea.
  • Play-test it. Then change something because of what you learned. That last step is the one people skip.

What to turn in

  1. Your sketch folder, zipped (all tabs).
  2. A screen recording of you playing your game, including winning or losing and then restarting.
  3. Your one-sentence game description.
  4. Your answer to Show your thinking below.

To zip your sketch: Sketch → Show Sketch Folder, go up one level, then right-click the sketch folder and compress it.
