What to do

Replace one-coin-at-a-time with a list.  Create many things with a counting loop, visit them with a loop, and collect them safely.

  • Declare an ArrayList, create it in setup(), and fill it with a counting loop.
  • Move and draw everything in it with a loop in draw() or playGame().
  • Collect items with collidesWith(), removing them with the backwards loop.
  • Decide what happens when the list is empty -- a new wave, a win, something of your own.
