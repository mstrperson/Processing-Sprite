Animate Your Character

Stop sliding a frozen drawing around. Make it move.

When to use this: Any time after Lesson 2 (you need a character to animate). It shines most during Lesson 9, when students want their character to feel alive. Pairs naturally with Bonus Lesson A — image frames animate the same way. One period.

This assignment is not scored. The stages below are a map of where you are and what to try next — you can revise and re-submit any time.

What to do

Bring your character to life with at least two poses flipping automatically.

  • Make a MyCharacterFrame class with a pose number, based on your Lesson 2 character.
  • Draw at least two poses that differ in one thing. Keep everything else identical.
  • Build an AnimatedSprite with FromSprite(...).AddFrameSprite(...), creating every frame at the same x and y.
  • Set a frame rate that suits the motion you are going for.
  • Move it in draw() — followMouse() or arrow keys, your choice.

What to turn in

  1. Your sketch folder, zipped (all tabs).
  2. A short screen recording — a screenshot cannot show an animation, so this one really needs to be a recording.
  3. Your answer to Show your thinking below.

To zip your sketch: Sketch → Show Sketch Folder, go up one level, then right-click the sketch folder and compress it.
