What to do

Give your game a voice. At minimum one sound effect, wired to the right moment in your code.

  • Install the Sound library and add import processing.sound.*;
  • Load at least one SoundFile in setup() from your data/ folder.
  • Play it with .play() at the exact spot the event happens — inside the if that detects it.
  • If you add music, loop it in setup() and turn the volume down so effects can be heard over it.
  • Choose sounds that fit your game. This is a design decision, not a technical one.

What to turn in

  1. Your sketch folder, zipped — including the data/ folder with your sound files, or nothing will run.
  2. A screen recording with audio so the sounds are actually audible.
  3. Your answer to Show your thinking below.

To zip your sketch: Sketch → Show Sketch Folder, go up one level, then right-click the sketch folder and compress it.

Standard

Getting StartedNo sound plays, or I get a null-pointer error on a SoundFile. Next step: install the Sound library (Sketch → Import Library → Add Library → "Sound"), and check the file is in data/ with an exact name — a null pointer means the file was not found.

Went Beyond…and I layered audio thoughtfully — music looping under one-shot effects with balanced volume (amp), milestone sounds, or guarded playback so rapid hits do not stutter.
