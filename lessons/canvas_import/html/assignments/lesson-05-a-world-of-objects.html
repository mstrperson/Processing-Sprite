What to do

Fill your character's world with things — created with a counting loop, drawn with a for-each loop.

  • Declare an ArrayList, create it in setup(), and fill it with a counting loop.
  • Move and draw everything in it with a for-each loop in draw().
  • Use random() so the objects are not all identical.
  • Make the world yours — the count, the colors, the sizes, and the speeds should say something about the game you are building.
  • Draw your player last so it appears on top.
