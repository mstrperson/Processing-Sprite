What to do

Get the Lesson 1 sketch running, then change your blob so it is unmistakably yours.

  • Run the starter sketch and get a Blob on screen.
  • Change at least one thing on purpose — color, size, position, or background.
  • Be ready to say why you chose it. "Because I like green" is a perfectly good reason. "I clicked randomly" is not.

What to turn in

  1. Your sketch folder, zipped.
  2. A screenshot of your sketch running.
  3. Your answer to Show your thinking below (type it in the comment box).

To zip your sketch: Sketch → Show Sketch Folder, go up one level, then right-click the sketch folder and compress it.

Where am I?

Standard
Create and display a Blob, using setup()/draw() and RGB color.
Getting StartedMy sketch is not running yet, or no blob appears. Next step: check the very first line. A missing import coxprogramming.processing.sprites.*; is the number one reason a sketch will not run. Fix it and run again.
Got It WorkingMy sketch runs and a Blob appears on screen.
Made It Mine ⭐…and I deliberately changed at least one thing — color, size, position, or background — and I can say why I chose it.
Went Beyond…and I added a second blob with its own color and place, or arranged several blobs into a small scene of my own.

⭐ Made It Mine is the goal for everyone. “Went Beyond” has no fixed list — the examples are only starting points. Going somewhere the lesson did not ask for is the whole idea.
