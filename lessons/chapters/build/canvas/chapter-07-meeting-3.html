7.6  Play-testing

Spend the first half of the meeting on what your play-testers found.  Watch them play, with your hands off the keyboard.  Whatever confuses them is a real problem.  Go fix it, then watch somebody play it again.

That cycle, build, test, change, test again, is what play-testing is.

7.7  Show it

Play a neighbor's game.  Tell them one thing you liked, and one thing you would add.

Then be ready to say two things about your own game:  what it is, in one sentence, and one decision you made on purpose.

Vocabulary

game loop -- draw() running over and over.  It's the engine of every game you've ever played.  (7.2)

play-testing -- playing your own game, or watching somebody else play it, to find out whether it's fun, and then changing it.  (7.6)

restart -- putting every variable back to its starting value, so the game can be played again.  (7.4)

win / lose condition -- the rules that decide when the game ends, and how.  (7.3)
